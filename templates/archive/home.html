A RECORD YOU CAN RETURN TO

From practical work to a citable version.

01

Show how you got there.

Keep methods, results or claims, artifacts, provenance, and limitations together. Another builder should be able to understand what happened.

02

Make the status clear.

Moderation checks scope, completeness, rights, privacy, safety, and abuse. It does not establish scientific validity.

03

Keep correction possible.

Published versions have stable pages and explicit history. A correction becomes a new version. Withdrawals remain visible as tombstones.

OPEN ELIGIBILITY, BOUNDED CAPACITY

Open to the people doing the work.

Independent builders, practitioners, and researchers are welcome. We are starting small enough for one founder to moderate responsibly. Human peer reviews and public vouches are not available in this release.
