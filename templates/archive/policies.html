ELIGIBILITY, EXPECTATIONS, AND CURRENT STATUS

A useful record. A clear set of boundaries.

Psyche Research is preparing a bounded pilot for research from practical work with AI systems. These draft policies need founder review before a live pilot. They are not legal advice or approved legal terms.

Who may contribute

Independent builders, practitioners, and researchers are eligible. University affiliation, employer prestige, GitHub activity, popularity, followers, reputation, and payment are not prerequisites. An account is not a publication promise.

Eligibility is open, but pilot capacity is not. During the bounded pilot, the founder creates accounts only for invited participants, chosen within the published scope and never by affiliation, popularity, payment or reputation. There is no public sign-up, request form or waitlist yet. Personal invitations can reflect the founder’s own network; that is a known fairness limit of the pilot. A neutral open request route is a decision for after the pilot.

We welcome inspectable experiments, observations, replications, negative results, datasets, tools, and well-supported ideas about AI systems. Explain what someone can learn or examine. Keep claims within the evidence.

Prepare a submission

  1. State a title, public contributor byline, research format, and abstract.
  2. Describe methods or link to enough detail to inspect them.
  3. Describe results or supported claims and their limitations.
  4. List artifact references, source provenance, and the selected publication license.
  5. Disclose AI assistance, confirm rights and contributor consent, and check for private data.

Save incomplete work as a private draft and return later. Before submitting, complete every required field and confirmation. This release accepts plain text and reference URLs. It does not accept uploaded files or run submitted code. The archive does not fetch or preview submitted links.

Choose external artifact hosts you trust and use durable versions where possible. A linked repository or artifact has its own access, retention, and licensing conditions.

What the labels mean

Publication
A particular version has a stable public record after a human moderation decision.
Human moderation
A check for scope, completeness, rights, privacy, safety, and abuse. It is not scientific validation or peer review.
Human peer review
Not available in this release. Future reviews must identify the exact version, scope, reviewer, and conflicts.
Public vouches
Not available in this release. Future vouches must identify the version and scope and allow correction and revocation.
Automated checks
Basic form completeness and application safeguards. They do not establish the truth, originality, reproducibility, or safety of a finding.

There is no verified independent review board, reviewer pool, accreditation, legal review, or guaranteed response time. Read the evidence and limitations before relying on a publication.

Human decisions and finite capacity

The founder schedules moderation within explicit queue and draft limits. Intake may be paused while existing drafts remain available. A moderator may request changes, decline a submission, or publish its exact frozen version, recording a reason. Moderators cannot approve their own work.

Decisions consider scope and operating safeguards, not a social score. A complete submission is eligible for consideration and is not automatically published. Spam, impersonation, unsafe disclosure, rights violations, and attempts to manipulate moderation may be declined or restricted.

Rights and privacy

Submit only material you have permission to publish, with the consent of named contributors. Do not include secrets, private credentials, nonconsensual personal data, confidential manuscripts, or material you cannot license. The publication license applies to the archive text; external artifacts may have different licenses.

Drafts, private decisions, appeals, and reports are accessible only to the relevant author and authorized humans as appropriate. Public pages contain only a deliberately published version. Private exports and backups also contain private material and must be protected. The operator must approve a retention and deletion schedule before real participant intake.

The application uses session cookies for sign-in and a security token to protect forms. There are no advertising trackers or social-profile scores in the pilot application. Operational security does not guarantee that external artifact hosts will protect your data.

Corrections and withdrawals

Published versions are fixed records. Propose a revision or correction with a change note; a human moderator must decide on the new version. Earlier versions do not silently inherit later claims, reviews, or endorsements.

Authors can withdraw a record, and authorized moderators can address urgent safety concerns. Withdrawal preserves a stable tombstone and history while suppressing manuscript content from public pages, search, and exports. Use a public withdrawal reason that does not repeat sensitive material.

Reports, conflicts, and appeals

Use the report form on a published record to raise a private concern. Use the appeal form on your submission to ask for reconsideration of a moderation decision. Include the relevant facts and the outcome you seek. A human records the response; no appeal response time is guaranteed.

Disclose conflicts and recuse where a decision cannot be impartial. If there is no unconflicted person available, pause the decision and explain the capacity limit. Familiar assistance is not an independent person and cannot resolve the conflict.

Optional familiar assistance

Familiars may help a contributor organize metadata, formatting, methods, and evidence. Disclose material assistance. You remain responsible for every statement and for permission to share the material.

Core submission and moderation work without an LLM. Familiars have no autonomous publication or appeal authority. Private submissions must not be sent to an external provider without explicit consent and a separately reviewed integration. Instructions inside submitted text are untrusted content.

No reputation gate

Reputation research is deferred and disabled in this release. GitHub activity, stars, followers, votes, and recommendations are biased and manipulable. Any future optional experiment must stay isolated in shadow mode, document cold-start, gaming, bias, and privacy risks, and have a way to disable and delete it. A score cannot decide publication, access, visibility, discovery order, or moderation priority.

Pause or wind-down

If the founder cannot sustain safe moderation, recovery or costs, intake pauses first and, where it is safe, authors keep access to their saved work. Closing the archive would follow a notice to affected contributors through an approved contact route, with a defined period to export their records. An emergency closure for harm could shorten that period, with the reason recorded. Pending submissions would be returned unpublished rather than declined on their merits.

Private drafts, unpublished submissions, decisions, appeals and reports would then be deleted or retained only as the adopted retention policy allows. Published versions and withdrawal tombstones would stay available as a minimal static record where that can be maintained safely; otherwise a closure notice says what was removed. Private material is not handed to another operator without consent; the hosting providers that run the service and disclosures required by law are matters for the privacy terms still to be adopted. No successor operator, permanent hosting or preservation guarantee is promised, so keep your own copy and deposit durable artifacts with an established archive.

These are draft commitments pending founder adoption. The founder runbook describes the operator procedure.
