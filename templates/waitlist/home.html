PSYCHE RESEARCHEST. 2026

Research can start anywhere

Built for people
who try things.

Independent builders, practitioners and researchers all have something to contribute. We’re preparing a human-moderated publication for original notes, thoughtful replications, tools and negative results.

No institutional affiliation, popularity or payment is a prerequisite. Clear methods, provenance and honest limitations matter.

01

Show the work.

Bring the question, the method and the evidence. Make it possible for someone else to understand what happened.

02

Name the limits.

A useful finding can be a small one. Uncertainty and results that didn’t go to plan belong in the record.

03

Leave room to revise.

We’re building toward stable versions, visible corrections and a clear distinction between moderation and scientific validation.
