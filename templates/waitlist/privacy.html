What joining means

Joining is optional. It creates no account, enables no research submission and promises no admission or endorsement. We don’t sell the list or publish who has registered. We collect no manuscripts, uploads or sensitive research material. We use no advertising trackers or analytics on these pages. A necessary security cookie protects the form; it does not identify a waitlist registration.

Storage and processors

Vercel serves the website and Neon stores the private list in managed PostgreSQL in the United States (Northern Virginia). Access is restricted to the operator and purpose-limited application and infrastructure roles. These providers process information under their service terms. Ordinary infrastructure request metadata may be processed by Vercel; the application does not log form contents, addresses, names or registration status. We send no automatic emails from this form.

Retention and deletion

Active registrations expire {{ retention_days }} days after collection unless you choose to register again after deletion. The operator runs daily expiry cleanup and removes requested records and managed exports within seven days of a verified request. Restricted disaster-recovery backups may retain a deleted record for up to seven additional days and are not used for contact. A restored database stays offline until expiry and recorded deletions are reapplied. Deletion-request records contain only a keyed address digest, request time and expiry, and expire after the recovery window. We keep no public registration status.

Your choices

You may ask to inspect, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, using the contact above. We verify control of the registered address before disclosing or changing a record. This waitlist does not automatically verify email ownership; someone else entering an address cannot change the existing registration. No updates will be sent without a separate operator check of permission and audience. The operator pauses collection if capacity, privacy or operating obligations cannot be met.
