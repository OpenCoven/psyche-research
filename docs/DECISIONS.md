## D009: durably consume rotating credentials before external exchange

Independent review of the earlier qualification repair reproduced consumed-token recovery after a process exit because replacement saving remained inside an outer request transaction. The candidate therefore uses a separate session/identity-bound WorkOSCredential table. It commits an empty refresh claim before exchange, validates the returned token and finalizes only the same live claim. Ordinary session response saving has no token authority. Dead claims require fresh sign-in after thirty seconds; active concurrent requests wait at most five seconds before a retryable response. Callback consumption remains durably committed before code exchange. Local process-loss, concurrent-request, stale-save, expiry and revocation regressions pass; PostgreSQL/provider evidence is still required.

Migration 0006 changes private credential storage and requires serialized rehearsal. Pre-existing in-session WorkOS tokens are not imported; those sessions require fresh sign-in. Raw SQLite recovery explicitly removes credentials before sessions, and ORM recovery removes credentials through the session cascade while preserving identity links. Reversing 0006 discards credential state. This is an engineering repair under the current candidate-preparation authorization; it grants no deployment, pilot, credential-creation or integration authority. ## D004: freeze, decide, publish and correct

Save uses an optimistic revision counter; stale tabs cannot overwrite accepted saves. Submitting freezes canonical JSON and SHA-256. Moderator decisions identify actor, exact revision/digest, scope (moderation only), reason, conflict declaration and time. Authors cannot moderate their own submission, including when they are moderators or superusers. An identical lost-response submit/decision retry is idempotent. Changes requests unfreeze through a new revision. A later publication creates a new immutable Version with a stable work/version URL and correction note.

Versions, decisions and audit events are append-only at both the model and SQLite trigger boundaries. Table-rebuilding migrations explicitly reinstall triggers in both directions; tests inspect the current schema and independent review exercised reverse/reapply. This protects ordinary application writes and direct UPDATE/DELETE, not a privileged database owner who can drop triggers.

An author or moderator can immediately withdraw a whole work with a public safe reason. All versions become tombstones, and manuscript content disappears from public HTML, discovery and JSON exports. Old external downloads/caches cannot be recalled. Raw private records remain in the restricted database according to the to-be-adopted retention policy. Restoring an older backup must reconcile takedowns, withdrawals and revoked access before any serving cutover.

## D011: founder operating model and the seven product principles

Recorded 2026-11-06 for [issue #2](https://github.com/OpenCoven/psyche-research/issues/2). Decision IDs D008–D010 are used by open draft PRs; this record takes the next free identifier and supersedes no earlier decision. It is a reversible implementation and drafting decision. Policy adoption, the operator, contact, cost cap, hosting, credentials, outreach and pilot opening remain human approval gates.

The issue asks implementers to preserve the seven product principles in the epic, but neither the epic body nor this repository enumerates them; the original brief is not in the repository. The epic outcome names six attributes: an accessible archive with open eligibility, bounded founder capacity, private intake, human moderation and stable versioned publication. Issue #2's acceptance criteria and the working instructions add non-gating reputation and optional assistance, and the epic scope adds safe input: no routine code execution, remote fetching, uploads, automatic publication, paid admission or fabricated research. For traceability, this working list folds those nine candidates into seven. The founder must confirm or correct it; a correction supersedes this record with a dated entry rather than rewriting it.

Confirmation, 2026-11-06: the founder confirmed this seven-principle list as written, answering a direct question in the agent working session, so it is now the project's principle list. Any later correction still supersedes it with a dated entry.

1. Accessible: the core contributor and reader path works with keyboard, zoom and reflow, screen readers and any writing script, without paid admission or an institutional account.
2. Open eligibility: anyone may submit under the same disclosed scope and capacity rules; affiliation, employer, GitHub, popularity, reputation, payment or recommendation are never prerequisites.
3. Bounded founder capacity: intake defaults paused, enforced limits apply equally to everyone, capacity is disclosed, and no response time is promised.
4. Private intake: drafts, submissions, decisions, appeals and reports stay private until an explicit human publication decision and never enter public pages, search, exports, logs, issues, previews or static bundles.
5. Human moderation with distinct trust records: publication, moderation, human peer review, public vouches and automated checks are separate records; moderation never implies scientific validity; reputation never gates eligibility, admission, priority, visibility, ranking or any label.
6. Stable versioned publication: published versions are immutable, corrections create new versions, withdrawals leave tombstones, and endorsements never carry over to a revision.
7. Optional assistance and safe input: the full workflow runs without an LLM; familiars have no publication, decision or appeal authority and no private production credentials; the archive never executes submitted code, fetches submitted links, accepts uploads or seeds fabricated research.

Select the smallest sustainable approach already recommended in D001 and [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md): a curated overlay with linked author-hosted artifacts, one founder as the only planned moderator, intake paused by default, configured limits of five queued submissions, one queued item and two active items per contributor and twenty active records overall, and no runtime LLM, uploads, fetching, code execution, reviews, vouches or reputation. The proposed weekly schedule and pause triggers are in [PILOT.md](PILOT.md); they are proposals, not accepted commitments. The public policies page renders the configured limits, the effective intake state and the per-field character limits from the running application, and describes the fixed one-queued-item rule and request-size limit, so those published numbers cannot drift from configuration.

Assumptions are a single founder with ordinary web skills, no reviewer pool, no independent board, no legal review, no funding commitment, no institutional accounts and no approved budget, contact, jurisdiction or hosting region. Exclusions are file custody, DOI minting, preservation guarantees, payments, public discussion, review matching, popularity ranking, reputation scoring, agent-triggered publication and any promised response time.

Cost drivers, in expected order, are founder moderation and correspondence time; managed PostgreSQL, hosting, backups and recovery drills; domain, TLS and monitoring; transactional email if adopted; incident response; and specialist policy or legal advice. Large artifacts and LLM inference are intentionally outside the critical path. No figure here is a vendor quote; the founder must approve an actual monthly cash cap before provisioning, and real spend and effort should be logged.

Contributor-facing admission, founder capacity, accessibility and wind-down are now explicit draft policies in [POLICIES.md](POLICIES.md), with the matching closure procedure in [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). New proposals needing founder adoption are the closure export period of at least 30 days, the recommendation that the founder not submit work during the pilot, WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a working target rather than a conformance claim, and a neutral open request route before intake is called public. Invitation-only pilot accounts are a recorded fairness limit. The former per-save size allowance was uneven across writing scripts.
