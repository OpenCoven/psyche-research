Production deployment, public launch, payments, new persistent credentials, external communications, destructive changes, accepting agreements and PR approvals require their applicable separate authorization. Do all safe preparation first so any requested approval concerns a concrete reviewable result. Keep intake paused until actual operating acceptance.

Write every prose paragraph as one logical source line in chat and rendered Markdown; blank lines separate paragraphs. Preserve semantic lists/tables/headings/fences, but remove artificial wrapping before publishing. Inspect the exact final serialized issue/PR/comment payload and use rendered preview when available. Do not publish mid-paragraph line breaks, trailing-double-space breaks or HTML breaks for ordinary prose.
